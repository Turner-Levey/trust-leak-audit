Timeline Signals

Green

At least two working weeks remain, Q&A is open or recently closed, mandatory steps are known, and final upload has a clear buffer.

Amber

The opportunity may be workable, but registration, addenda, attachments, pricing, or review windows need verification before proposal hours are assigned.

Red

Q&A is missed, required setup or site visits are at risk, or too few working days remain for compliance, pricing, review, and upload.
