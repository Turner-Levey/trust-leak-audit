April 29, 2026

Free toolkit launch package

The current public release packages the Trust Leak Audit into a no-signup site with calculator pages, sample reports, a Vercel incident response checklist, export actions, local event capture, and share-ready page metadata.

Trust and conversion tools

Use the landing-page trust score, ecommerce trust checklist, ROAS calculator, paid traffic break-even calculator, local service lead value calculator, LTV:CAC calculator, SaaS payback calculator, lead funnel calculator, and agency margin calculator.

Vercel incident checklist

The Vercel security incident response checklist gives teams a browser-only, source-backed way to track environment-variable rotation, activity-log review, 2FA, and deployment protection steps.

Shareable proof assets

The sample report gallery includes anonymized SaaS, ecommerce, agency, and local-service examples with copyable snippets. Public pages include share-ready metadata and no-signup paths into the highest-value tools.

Public filing trigger briefs

The SEC Filing Trigger Briefs hub packages fifteen source-linked Form 8-K examples across transaction, auditor-change, and leadership-change buyer routes. The pages are operational signal examples, not investment advice.

LLMS.txt cache checker

The cache freshness checker compares a live origin `llms.txt` file with a directory or cached copy, then reports missing priority URLs for crawl-review and launch evidence.

Public bid no-bid calculator

The public-bid triage tool starts the next public-data experiment by scoring RFP, RFQ, and bid opportunities against fit, pursuit cost, deadline risk, and expected value.

Public bid deadline planner

The deadline planner turns RFP, RFQ, Q&A, registration, site-visit, draft, review, and final upload dates into a copyable proposal workback plan.

Form D deadline calculator

The Form D calculator starts the next public-data tool sprint by turning a Regulation D first-sale date into a source-linked filing target, EDGAR access note, annual amendment reminder, memo, and CSV row.
