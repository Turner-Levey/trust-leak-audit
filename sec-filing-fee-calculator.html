How To Use The Fee Number Safely

1. Confirm The Rate Window

This page was last checked on May 4, 2026. Recheck the SEC rate page before October 1, 2026 and before any filing deadline.

2. Verify The Fee Table

Use the copied row as a review aid only. Build and validate the actual EX-FILING-FEES exhibit inside EDGAR, FEPT, filing software, or counsel-approved tooling.

3. Check The Account

The SEC notes acceptance may be delayed when fee funds are missing, insufficient because of a calculation error, or wired with incorrect information.

4. Track Late-Day Filing Effects

SEC EDGAR fee guidance describes special treatment for some fee-related filings received after 5:30 p.m. ET and before 10 p.m. ET.
