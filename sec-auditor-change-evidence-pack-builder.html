Evidence Workflow

1. Open The SEC Source

Use a public SEC filing page, archive filing, or Exhibit 16.1 source. Copy the source URL into the builder before adding operational notes.

2. Separate Facts From Inferences

Record only disclosed auditor transitions, disagreement statements, reportable events, and control language. Put unknowns into the next-step field.

3. Choose A Buyer Route

Map the filing to an operational research route such as audit-transition advisory, SOX support, technical accounting, governance, or public-company research.

4. Export The Pack

Copy the Markdown note for review, or copy the CSV row into a watchlist. Keep every row tied to the SEC source link and filing date.
